Preparing for system design interviews is crucial for landing senior engineering roles at top tech companies. But when it comes to practice, the cost difference between AI and human mock interviews is staggering—and it might change how you approach your interview prep.

## The Human Mock Interview: Premium Pricing

Traditional human-led mock interviews typically cost around **$200 per session**. Platforms like interviewing.io, Pramp (for expert sessions), and independent coaches charge anywhere from $150 to $300 for a single one-hour system design mock interview.

What do you get for $200? An experienced interviewer, usually a current or former engineer from FAANG companies, who will:
- Present you with a system design problem
- Evaluate your approach and solutions
- Provide personalized feedback
- Answer questions about their experience

While valuable, the premium price creates a significant barrier. Most candidates need multiple practice sessions to feel confident—at $200 per session, five practice interviews would cost $1,000. For many job seekers, especially those early in their careers or between jobs, this investment is simply out of reach.

## The AI Alternative: Democratizing Interview Prep

AI-powered system design mock interviews have emerged as a game-changing alternative, with costs ranging from **$3 to $12 per session**. Some platforms offer subscription models at $10-30 per month for unlimited practice, effectively bringing the per-session cost to just a few dollars.

Modern AI interviewers leverage advanced language models to:
- Generate diverse system design problems
- Engage in realistic back-and-forth dialogue
- Ask clarifying questions like a real interviewer
- Provide instant, detailed feedback on your solutions
- Available 24/7 with no scheduling required

## The Cost Comparison: 20x-60x Difference

Let's break down the math:

**Traditional Human Approach:**
- 5 practice sessions × $200 = $1,000
- Scheduling hassle and limited availability
- One-time feedback per session

**AI-Powered Approach:**
- 5 practice sessions × $5 (average) = $25
- Or: $20/month subscription for unlimited practice
- Immediate availability, practice anytime
- Iterate and retry as many times as needed

The AI approach costs **20 to 60 times less** than human mock interviews. This dramatic price difference means you can practice exponentially more, building muscle memory for common patterns and gaining confidence through repetition.

## What About Quality?

The natural question: can AI really match human interviewers? While AI may not perfectly replicate the nuance of human interaction, modern AI interviewers are surprisingly effective at:
- Covering standard system design patterns
- Identifying gaps in your approach
- Providing structured feedback on scalability, reliability, and trade-offs
- Simulating the pressure of a real interview

For most candidates, the ability to practice 20-40 times with AI for the same cost as 1-2 human sessions creates far more value. You can use AI for volume practice and reserve expensive human sessions for final validation before actual interviews.

## The Bottom Line

At $3-12 per session versus $200, AI-powered system design mock interviews represent a fundamental shift in accessibility. The cost barrier to quality interview preparation has essentially disappeared. Whether you're a student, career switcher, or experienced engineer looking to level up, AI mock interviews offer a practical, affordable path to interview readiness.

The question is no longer whether you can afford to practice—it's how many sessions you'll complete before your next big interview.
